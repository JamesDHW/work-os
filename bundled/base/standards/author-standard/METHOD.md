1. Load the writing-standards skill.
2. Ask the user, with ask_user, the questions you need: what the output is, who receives it, what makes it good or bad, and which tools or services the work touches. Ask one focused question at a time.
3. Read one or two existing standards with workos.standard.read to match the workspace's style.
4. Draft STANDARD.md and METHOD.md. Keep criteria observable and short.
5. Save with workos.standard.save. The user sees the exact text in the approval and can edit it before approving.
6. Call complete with a summary of the standard and the choices you made.
